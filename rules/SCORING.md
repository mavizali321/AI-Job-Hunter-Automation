# Job Scoring Rubric

| Category | Points |
|---|---:|
| Role relevance | 30 |
| Technical match | 25 |
| Experience fit | 15 |
| Location/work authorization fit | 15 |
| Company quality/opportunity | 10 |
| Entry-level accessibility | 5 |
| Total | 100 |

## Role relevance
30 = exact/near-exact Forward Deployed / AI deployment
25 = AI integration / automation / solutions
20 = AI software / applied AI
15 = adjacent software role with substantial AI work
0 = unrelated

## Technical match
25 = strong overlap with LLM/API/agentic + software integration
20 = strong overlap with AI integration plus some gaps
15 = mixed match
10 = mostly frontend with limited AI relevance
0–5 = major mismatch

## Location
15 = Karachi onsite/hybrid or Pakistan remote
12 = worldwide remote explicitly accepting Pakistan
6 = ambiguous worldwide remote
0 = explicitly excludes Pakistan and no route

## Decision
90+ PRIORITY APPLY
80–89 APPLY
70–79 REVIEW
<70 SKIP
